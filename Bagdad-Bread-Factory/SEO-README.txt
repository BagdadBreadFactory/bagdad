# Google Search / SEO additions

This build adds:
- valid HTML document structure on the homepage
- stronger title/description/robots/canonical metadata
- Open Graph and Twitter preview metadata
- a 640x640 square favicon using the existing logo
- JSON-LD for the bakery, website, breadcrumbs, categories, menu categories, and branches
- `max-image-preview:large` for image eligibility
- `noindex, follow` on customer order utility pages
- an updated sitemap last-modified date

Important:
- These changes make the site easier for Google to crawl and understand; they do NOT guarantee an exact Google result layout, sitelinks, images, or ranking.
- Google decides whether to show sitelinks, images, local panels, and other search features.
- For the local business panel/Maps listing, the business should also have a correctly managed Google Business Profile with matching real-world business information.
- After deployment, verify the live homepage and sitemap in Google Search Console and request indexing. Google may take days or weeks to recrawl a site.
